Even highly accurate AI systems are not infallible, and their reliability varies from case to case. To make good decisions, users therefore need to know when to trust an AI system's prediction and when to override it. I study **human–AI decision-making** in high-stakes contexts such as clinical diagnosis, with a particular focus on how the **uncertainty of AI-generated predictions** can be communicated to human decision-makers to support appropriate reliance and improve decision quality.
